FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Idea Sprint 2026, team composition, guidelines, and bounties.

What is the team size requirement?

Teams can consist of 1 to 4 members. You can participate solo or form a full squad across different engineering branches.

Is there any registration fee?

No! Idea Sprint 2026 is completely free for all selected student innovators, including meals, workstation power, and hardware support during the 24 hours.

What should we bring to the venue?

Bring your laptops, chargers, hardware kits (if building IoT/Robotics), student IDs, and lots of enthusiasm. Sleeping gear and high-speed Wi-Fi will be available on-site.

How do the judging and bounties work?

Projects are evaluated on originality, technical complexity, utility, and live demo quality by a distinguished jury of founders, venture capitalists, and senior engineers.
